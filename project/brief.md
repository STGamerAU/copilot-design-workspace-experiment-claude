# Product Brief

## Problem

A small-business customer has an upcoming technician appointment for a new internet service.

Their circumstances have changed and they need to reschedule the appointment.

The existing experience does not exist for this experiment. Treat this as a greenfield product-design problem.

## User

A small-business owner or manager responsible for arranging the service installation.

## Desired outcome

The user can confidently move their appointment to another available time and understand that the change has been successfully made.

## Initial scope

The experience should support:

- viewing the existing appointment
- viewing available alternatives
- selecting another appointment
- reviewing the change
- confirming it
- seeing the confirmed new appointment

## Out of scope

- authentication
- payments
- cancelling the service
- technician tracking
- installation management after the appointment
- production backend integration

## Project goal

Create a small interactive prototype suitable for testing the core rescheduling experience.
